# Tuesday 17 November 2026 — The World in One Day

An infographic summing up the world news of Tuesday 17 November 2026, built for the make.fun **news-image-20261117** bounty. The image names the day it sums up and covers six stories from that day, across regions and fields:

1. **Kerch Bridge hit** — Ukraine strikes the bridge to Crimea; a road span falls, and Russia hits Kyiv's bridges in reply. (BBC)
2. **Iran fires drones at a US base** — drones target a US radar site in Bahrain; the attack is intercepted, no casualties. (Reuters)
3. **Trump lands in Manila** — the US president opens an Asia trip for the ASEAN summit, ahead of talks with Xi and Putin. (AP)
4. **Jets scrambled over Japan** — Japan sends fighters up after suspected Russian aircraft violate its airspace; South Korea does the same. (BBC)
5. **Ebola out of control** — DR Congo outbreak: 5,500 dead and 11,200 infected, Africa CDC says. (Africa CDC)
6. **Nvidia reports** — first earnings since Jensen Huang stepped down, after Tuesday's closing bell. (Level Headlines)

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024, drawn with PIL during the build) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). No network, fully deterministic — every run writes the same bytes.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
